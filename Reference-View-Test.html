Source, privacy and prototype limits
Color rendering uses Aladin Lite 3.8.2 (CDS), LGPL-3.0-or-later, with its logo retained. Tile data credits: Digitized Sky Survey / STScI / NASA, colored and HiPS prepared by CDS/CNRS/Unistra; mirrors credited with each image. Your working image is never passed to the renderer.
NASA SkyView / HEASARC delivers the DSS2 Red reference as a grayscale image. See SkyView for survey information. NASA mode is not a color reference. CDS service status and alternatives.
Survey: DSS2 color, STScI / photographic survey contributors; service: CDS, Strasbourg. See HiPS survey information and HiPS2FITS service for details. Survey colors, resolution, epoch and coverage differ from your capture. Check the source’s terms before redistributing imagery.
Nothing is uploaded from the left panel. Online access occurs only after you check consent and press Load. There are no analytics, API keys, accounts or background image searches. Local working files stay in browser memory. If Save is enabled, up to five downloaded color references, coordinates and source credits are stored in this browser for offline reuse; local working/reference files are never saved by this feature. Clear saved references removes only that cache. Storage availability varies by browser. Requests may also use the normal HTTP cache. Reloading clears your working image.
FITS: uncompressed, primary-image 2D monochrome or three-plane RGB only; BITPIX 8, 16, 32, −32 and −64. BSCALE/BZERO supported. Preview is sampled to 1600 pixels with an approximate asinh stretch. Raw Bayer files are shown as monochrome without debayering; compressed, extension-only and other FITS layouts require the Windows app. No prediction, automatic editing, color calibration, stacking or AI reconstruction is performed.


Standalone evaluation only. Easy Astro Studio’s installed program, projects and settings are untouched. For a future integrated version, object name or solved sky coordinates could suggest the reference, and a licensed cache could avoid repeated downloads.
