Source, privacy and prototype limits

Survey: DSS2 color, STScI / photographic survey contributors; service: CDS, Strasbourg. See HiPS survey information and HiPS2FITS service for details. Survey colors, resolution, epoch and coverage differ from your capture. Check the source’s terms before redistributing imagery.

Nothing is uploaded from the left panel. Online access occurs only after you check consent and press Load. There are no analytics, API keys, accounts or background image searches. Local files stay in browser memory and are not saved in browser storage. Requests may use the browser’s normal HTTP cache. Reloading clears your local selections.

FITS: uncompressed, primary-image 2D monochrome or three-plane RGB only; BITPIX 8, 16, 32, −32 and −64. BSCALE/BZERO supported. Preview is sampled to 1600 pixels with an approximate asinh stretch. Raw Bayer files are shown as monochrome without debayering; compressed, extension-only and other FITS layouts require the Windows app. No prediction, automatic editing, color calibration, stacking or AI reconstruction is performed.

Standalone evaluation only. Easy Astro Studio’s installed program, projects and settings are untouched. For a future integrated version, object name or solved sky coordinates could suggest the reference, and a licensed cache could avoid repeated downloads.
