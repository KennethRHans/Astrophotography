# Privacy information draft — owner review required before publication

This draft is not a published privacy policy. The operator must confirm its
contact details, message retention/deletion process and policy URL before use.

## Current application behavior

- FITS inputs, processing, previews, temporary aligned frames, exports and .astro
  projects are handled on the user's device. Astronomy images are not uploaded
  automatically. The app has no built-in advertising or usage-analytics SDK.
- Theme preference is stored locally per Windows user. Projects can contain
  original input filenames/paths as references and embed the loaded linear image.
- If a user chooses Report a Bug / Suggest a Fix and clicks Send, the app sends
  the entered message, report type, app name/version and optional name/email to
  Formspree endpoint https://formspree.io/f/xzezdzwd over HTTPS for delivery to
  the developer. It does not attach images or processing logs automatically.
  Formspree may process network metadata under its own terms/privacy policy.
- Buy the Developer a Coffee opens https://cash.app/$KennethRHans externally.
  Planning opens https://planner.astrivanta.com externally. Those sites have
  their own privacy practices. The app does not receive payment-card information.
- Microsoft Store distribution/diagnostics are subject to Microsoft's terms and
  the publisher's Partner Center choices; this app does not implement a separate
  telemetry collector.

## Owner decisions required

1. Confirm the legal/operator name and a public support/privacy contact method.
2. Confirm how long submitted feedback is kept and how deletion requests are
   handled, including Formspree/mailbox retention.
3. Publish the approved policy at a stable HTTPS URL, then add it to the app and
   Store listing before submission.
4. Review region-specific disclosures applicable to the intended markets.

No retention promises, legal identity or public privacy URL have been invented
or approved by this preparation kit.
